    03

    How work moves through the factory.

    Six rules, written down and enforced by config. Each dot below is one change: written by one model family, reviewed by the other, verified against the running build, then shipped.

    written by Claude written by GPT ring = reviewer from the other family active rule

    The orchestrator never leaves the chat.

    The model I talk to plans, routes, and reviews. It doesn't disappear into a forty-minute solo implementation.

    Research and implementation get fanned out.

    Wide reading goes to a subagent that returns a conclusion, not a file dump. Independent work runs in parallel.

    Model choice is a routing decision.

    Hard architecture and reviews to the top tier, lead work to Sol, ordinary to Terra, mechanical to Luna.

    One independent review, other family.

    A model that didn't write it gets the intent, the diff, and the evidence. Read-only, fresh context, high effort.

    Verify against the running build.

    Reproduce first. Exercise the real flow in a real browser or binary. Tests are evidence, not the whole case.

    “Fix this” means finished.

    Implement, verify, review, push, CI green, PR ready. And the inverse: a question authorizes nothing.
