George Nijo

About

George Nijo is a software engineer based in Boston, Massachusetts. He builds infrastructure for AI agents — the layer that sits between individual AI coding assistants and the work they do together: coordination meshes that let agents share files and answers without stepping on each other, MCP brokers that give every agent one audited front desk for tool calls, and control planes that schedule long-lived agents, enforce budgets, and keep kill switches within reach.

His current focus is AgentOS, a personal and home agent operating system: one control plane for the agents that run his code, his inbox, and eventually the house.

Principles

  • Local-first — build tools that solve your own problems on your own machine.
  • Honesty-first — then open-source the parts worth sharing.
  • Ship the menu bar app before the platform.

Languages

Python, Go, Rust, and Swift, with a dash of TypeScript for dashboards.
